1️⃣ Setup & Status Check

Before play begins, verify your player profile:

  • Enter your Phone Number on this tab so the app remembers you on future visits.
  • Verify that your Group (Mens, Womens, or Mixed) and Status (Active) are set correctly.
2️⃣ Player Check-In Rules

On play days, head to the Check-In tab:

  • 📍 Location Limit: Check-in can ONLY be done on-site at Marinette.
  • ⏰ Time Restriction: Check-in opens strictly AFTER 10:00 AM.
  • 👥 Checking In Others: You may check in yourself as well as any other present players in your group.
  • ⏳ Court Assignment Delay: Captains may wait until check-in completes at 10:50 AM before assigning final courts.
3️⃣ Court Assignments & Entering Scores

Once courts are scheduled, open the Enter Scores tab:

  • Your phone number will automatically pull up your assigned Court Number, Foursome, and 3 Games.
  • Court Not Showing Yet? Tap the "Recheck for Foursome Assignment" button to fetch the latest schedule.
  • 📝 Submitting Scores: Only ONE player from your foursome needs to enter and submit scores for the court.
